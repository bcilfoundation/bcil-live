## Full description
BCIL Live is the home of the BCIL Foundation community: students, mentors and alumni building integrity, leadership and excellence together.

LIVE SESSIONS
Join BCIL live audio sessions from anywhere. Listen in, react, comment, and request to call in and speak with the host, podcast-style.

CAMPUS SYNC AND GROUPS
Your Campus Sync chapter has its own page: upcoming activities with RSVPs, updates from your leaders, the member list and a group chat. Cohort and community groups keep everyone connected between programmes.

CHAT AND FRIENDS
Chat with the BCIL community, add friends, and message them directly. Under-18 members can only connect with BCIL staff and mentors, and every space is moderated.

DAILY QUOTES
A short, thoughtful quote every day on integrity, mentorship, persistence, impact, responsibility and excellence, with a question to reflect on.

THE LIBRARY
Watch BCIL talks and programme videos, save your favourites and take notes.

PROGRAMMES AND FORMS
Register for programmes such as Finding Campus Balance and stay up to date on what's next.

BCIL Foundation LBG is a non-profit organisation working with students in Ghana.

## Google Play: Data safety answers
- Data collected: Name, Email address, Phone number (if used to sign in), Photos (profile and group photos, optional), Messages (in-app messages), Audio (voice in live calls; streamed, not stored), Other user-generated content (posts, comments, form responses).
- Purpose: App functionality, Account management. Not used for advertising or analytics.
- Data shared with third parties: No (the services we use process data on our behalf, which Google does not count as sharing).
- Encrypted in transit: Yes.
- Users can request deletion: Yes, in the app (profile menu → Delete my account) and via the deletion page above.
